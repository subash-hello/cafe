Our Process

From Farm to Cup

Every decision matters. Here's where the magic happens.

01

Sourcing

Annual visits to partner farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and beyond. Cupping lots, understanding flavour potential, negotiating fair prices.

02

Arrival & Resting

Green beans arrive at ONA's Canberra roastery. We rest them 2-4 weeks to allow moisture levels to stabilise before profiling.

03

Roasting

Each origin is roasted individually. Light roasts highlight terroir. Medium roasts balance complexity with sweetness. Every decision documented.

04

Resting & Extraction

Roasted coffee rests 12-48 hours before sale. We dial espresso daily. Every cup benefits from this patience.
