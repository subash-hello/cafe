Who We Are

Welcome to
The Cupping Room

Nestled in the heart of Canberra's University Ave, The Cupping Room is a gathering place for coffee lovers, brunch enthusiasts, and anyone who appreciates something made with genuine care. Following a thoughtful renovation, we've welcomed a fresh chapter while staying true to everything that made us a community favourite.

We are a proud part of the ONA Coffee family — one of Australia's most celebrated specialty roasters. That means every espresso, every filter, every carefully steamed milk is built on a foundation of direct relationships with farmers across the world's great coffee-growing regions.

Our seasonal brunch menu evolves with Australia's produce calendar, complementing our coffee with dishes that are honest, delicious, and unforgettable. From your morning flat white to a long Sunday shakshuka, we're here to make your visit worth lingering over.

— The Cupping Room Team

What Drives Us

Our Values

Three principles that guide everything we do — from how we source our beans to how we welcome you through the door.

☕

Craft Without Compromise

Every variable matters. We dial in our espresso daily, source our beans with obsessive care, and train our baristas to championship standard — because great coffee demands it.

🌿

Community First

The Cupping Room exists because of Canberra. We invest in local producers, support our neighbourhood, and create a space where everyone feels genuinely welcome.

🌍

Transparency & Sustainability

We know our farmers by name. We pay above market prices. We believe the story behind a coffee is as important as what's in the cup — and we'll share it freely.

How We Got Here

A Decade of
Shared Moments

From a small espresso bar to a landmark Canberra café — each year has brought new friendships, new coffees, and new reasons to gather.

2012

The First Pour

The Cupping Room opens its doors on University Ave as part of the ONA Coffee family — with a small menu and a big vision for specialty coffee in Canberra.

2015

Growing the Community

Expanded seating, a new seasonal brunch menu, and the start of our first public cupping sessions draw regulars from across the ACT.

2018

Barista Championship Recognition

Our team begins competing nationally, bringing home medals and a new level of craft to everything we serve at the counter.

2022

The Renovation

A thoughtful redesign transforms the space — warm timbers, considered lighting, and a layout built around the flow of community life.

2024

The Next Chapter

A fresh look, an expanded events programme, and the same familiar faces behind the bar. The best is still to come.

Our Coffee Family

Part of the
ONA Legacy

ONA Coffee is one of Australia's most lauded specialty roasteries, with a worldwide reputation built on direct relationships with farmers, obsessive attention to quality, and a genuine passion for the stories behind every bean.

As part of the ONA family, The Cupping Room has access to some of the most extraordinary lots on the planet — coffees from Ethiopia's highlands, Colombia's micro-lots, and beyond — roasted metres away at ONA's Canberra roastery.

This connection means you're never more than a few steps from the source. Every cup carries that lineage.
