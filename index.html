Our Story

Where Every Cup Tells a Story

The Cupping Room began with a simple belief: great coffee should be accessible, understood, and celebrated. Founded in the heart of Canberra, we've spent over a decade building relationships with farmers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

We obsess over every variable — from soil elevation to roast curve — so that what lands in your cup is nothing short of extraordinary. Our seasonal menu changes with intention, reflecting the rhythms of Australian produce and the evolving landscape of specialty coffee.

More than a café, we're a gathering place — for conversations that matter, work that inspires, and moments that linger long after the last sip.

— The Cupping Room Team

The Process

Coffee Brewed
with Intention

From the moment a coffee cherry is picked to the final pour, we're involved at every stage. No shortcuts. No compromises.

01

Direct Farm Sourcing

We visit our farm partners annually across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, paying above market premiums for exceptional lots.

02

In-House Roasting

Our head roaster profiles each origin to highlight its best attributes — light, transparent roasts that let the terroir speak.

03

Precision Extraction

Every espresso is dialled in daily. Every filter brew is timed to the second. Your cup is the result of obsessive care.
